# Sample case — balcony door power (synthetic)

**Synthetic DIY scene.** No real address, order, or host path.

## Scene

Small inner balcony used as a short paint workspace. Power from the adjoining room outlet. Door should stay effectively closed for heat and drafts. Load target: **microwave-tier** (~1–1.5 kW class) — not dryer/heater-bank.

## Constraints

- Do not drill the sash if avoidable  
- Do not leave the door ajar  
- Cord must not be crushed in the weatherseal  
- Single heavier tool or similar draw — not parallel heaters  

## Verdict

**Conditional OK (microwave-tier):** short 10–15A extension + weatherstrip (or forced-vent strip) pass; keep the door from chewing the jacket; no ultra-slim gadget cord as the only conductor.

## Keywords

- `강제환기 전용 문풍지`
- `문풍지` / `창문 틈새막이`
- `연장선 15A`
- optional: `창문형 에어컨 패널`

## Shortlist (classes)

| # | Class | Why |
|---|-------|-----|
| 1 | Forced-vent / wedge weatherstrip | Holds a slight opening and seals the rest |
| 2 | Short 15A extension | Matches microwave-tier draw |
| 3 | Floor cord cover (balcony side) | Trip + abrasion |

## Don’t

- Door propped open all day  
- Coiled reel under load  
- Ribbon “under-door” novelty cord as sole microwave-tier feed  
- Heater + tool on one strip  

## Decision

Research pattern only — operator buys to local stock.
